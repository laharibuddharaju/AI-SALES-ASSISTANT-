# Example Chat Queries

### 1. View Leads
- `Show me all leads`
- `Get my leads`

### 2. Create a Lead
- `Create a lead with email bob@acme.com`
- `Add a new lead with email sarah@techcorp.com, name Sarah Smith, company Tech Corp, and product code PRD-456`

### 3. Update a Lead
- `Update the lead with email john@acme.com to have the name John Doe, company Acme Corp, and product code PRD-123`
- `Change the lead with email bob@acme.com to have the status qualified and deal value $1000`
- `Update the lead with email bob@acme.com status to qualified`

### 4. Delete a Lead
- `Delete the lead with email bob@acme.com`
